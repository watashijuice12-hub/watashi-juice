# Watashi Juice

**Your daily dose of freshness.**

Watashi Juice is a fruit juice e-commerce website prototype developed as part of the **BUC3106 course project at Makerere University Business School (MUBS)**.

The website presents a colourful selection of fruit juices through a simple, modern and responsive design. It allows visitors to explore juice flavours, view product information, understand the proposed ordering process and learn about the Watashi Juice concept.

## Website Features

- **Home:** Introduces Watashi Juice, highlights featured products and explains the ordering concept.
- **Products:** Displays fruit juices with descriptions, categories, bottle sizes and prices in Ugandan shillings (UGX).
- **About Us:** Explains the Watashi Juice concept, values and inspiration.
- **How to Pay:** Outlines the proposed ordering process, payment options and delivery arrangements.
- **Policies:** Provides information about the website's terms, privacy and other relevant policies.
- **Responsive Design:** Supports desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

## Product Categories

The website features four main product categories:

- Classics
- Blends
- Smoothies
- Wellness

Products are presented in 500ml and 1L bottle sizes.

## Important Disclaimer

This website was created for educational and demonstration purposes only. Watashi Juice is presented as a student business concept and is not operating as a verified commercial online store.

The website does not process real orders, collect actual payments or provide live delivery services. Product prices, ordering instructions and payment methods are included to demonstrate how an e-commerce website could operate.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how a small fruit juice business can use an e-commerce website to showcase its products, communicate with potential customers and present a clear online ordering experience.

The project also demonstrates practical web development skills, responsive website design and digital business presentation.
