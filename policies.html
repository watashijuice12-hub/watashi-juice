Our policies

Simple, plain
and fair.

These proposed policies explain the principles behind the Watashi Juice student demonstration website.

POLICY GUIDE

Delivery PolicyReturns and RefundsPrivacy PolicyTerms and Conditions
01

Delivery Policy

  • Delivery may be arranged within Nakawa and nearby areas of Kampala, depending on availability.
  • Orders and delivery arrangements are discussed during our stated working hours, 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
  • Delivery time, location and any delivery charges are confirmed before an order is accepted.
  • Customers are encouraged to refrigerate their juice after receiving it and enjoy it while fresh.
02

Returns and Refunds

  • Because our juices are perishable, returns of opened products are generally not accepted for a change of mind.
  • If a juice arrives damaged, incorrect or in poor condition, customers should contact Watashi Juice as soon as possible, preferably on the day of delivery.
  • We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate solution, such as a replacement or refund, where applicable.
  • Any approved refund would be arranged through an agreed payment method.
03

Privacy Policy

  • We respect the privacy of everyone who visits the Watashi Juice website.
  • The website does not require visitors to create an account or enter payment details.
  • Customers may choose to contact Watashi Juice through WhatsApp using the contact button provided.
  • Information shared through WhatsApp will only be used to respond to customer questions and assist with order requests.
  • Customers should never be asked to share their Mobile Money PINs, passwords or other sensitive financial information.
04

Terms and Conditions

  • Product descriptions, bottle sizes and displayed prices are provided to help customers understand the proposed Watashi Juice menu.
  • Product availability, order quantities and final costs should be confirmed before an order is accepted.
  • The proposed payment methods are MTN Mobile Money and Cash on Delivery, as explained on the How to Pay page.
  • Delivery or collection arrangements should be agreed upon before an order is confirmed.
  • Customers should contact Watashi Juice directly if they have questions about products, payments or delivery.
